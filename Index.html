KNOTZ FROG HOP

Hop the highway. Ride the river. Fill all 5 lily pads across 3 lands — Meadow, Dusk Rapids, Night Highway.

HOW TO PLAY

  • ↑↓←→ or WASD to hop. Swipe or d-pad works on touch.
  • Dodge cars on the road. Ride logs and turtles across the river — sunk turtles don't count!
  • Land on all 5 glowing lily pads to clear the land. Grab the fly for +200.
  • Up-hops score with FLOW multipliers for chained hops. Pads pay 150+ and grow as they fill — banking beats farming. You have 5 lives, plus a bonus life per land cleared. P pauses, M mutes.
  • Beat the clock: each crossing attempt is timed. Run out and the frog loses a life. Held keys and d-pad repeat hops.
  • Chain quick hops to build FLOW up to x5 hop points. Sitting still scores nothing.
